      Voices of the movement

      The people pushing for change

      • Fridays For Future Ukraine

        Ilyess El Korbi

        Helped register Ukraine's first climate protests in 2019 and now serves as board secretary of Fridays For Future Ukraine, raising awareness from Berlin.

      • Green Generation Initiative

        Elizabeth Wanjiru Wathuti

        Award-winning Kenyan activist whose initiative has planted over 30,000 tree seedlings and inspires young people to care for nature.

      • Fridays for Future

        Greta Thunberg

        A solo school protest that became an international strike of millions. Sailed the Atlantic for the 2019 UN climate conference.

      • Writer & speaker

        Tori Tsui

        Bristol-based climate justice activist from Hong Kong and New Zealand, campaigning for reparative action that benefits the most marginalised.

      • Kerala, India

        John Paul Jose

        Environmental activist working with NGOs and the UN to highlight how global warming is affecting India's forests and ecosystems.

      • Philippines

        Climate Change Commission

        The country's lead policy body on climate change, building adaptive capacity and steering toward a climate-resilient, low-carbon economy.

      • Climate justice

        Dominique Palmer

        Climate justice activist and University of Birmingham student, active in the youth-led Fridays for Future movement.

      • Clean Earth Gambia

        Fatou Jeng

        Youth climate activist in Gambia focused on education, conservation and tree planting, and founder of Clean Earth Gambia.
